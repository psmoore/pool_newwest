Lap Pool lane schedule

Fall · September 21 – December 18, 2026

The eight-lane 50 m Lap Pool, lane by lane. Pick a day to see how many lanes are open for length swim, and whether the pool is set up long course (50 m) or short course (25 m).

Live from Pool Relay · open the full calendar →

Long course?Monday, Wednesday and Friday mornings: all eight 50 m lanes 5:30–6am, then four until 9:30am (8:30am Wednesday). The rest of the week is 25 m.
The most lanes?All eight short-course lanes late morning to 2pm on weekdays (from 7:30am Tuesday and Thursday), and Saturday 6–10pm.
After work?Only three lanes 5–7:30pm Monday, Wednesday and Friday (4–7pm Tuesday and Thursday). It opens up to six or eight lanes after that.

“Turn to landscape view”

“Is there a long-course lane at 7am on Friday?”

On the centre's page the answer is in a seven-column table that tells phone users to turn their phone sideways to read it. Each cell carries a time, a lane count and a course, so a swimmer has to read across and down to find one morning.

Here it's one tap on the day, with the open lanes drawn across the pool and the course shown on each block. It reads the same on a phone. When the schedule changes for a meet, a holiday or a closure, the aquatics staff change it once in an AI chat, and every page that shows it updates.

Questions for the aquatics team

Everything on these calendars comes from what the centre publishes. These are the places where a detail isn't published, where two notices disagree, or where we had to make a call.

  • GapIs the tank bulkheaded into two 25 m courses?
    The grid never shows more than eight lanes at 25 m, so the calendar has one eight-lane short course. If there is a second course, what uses it?
  • ConflictWhen did the fall schedule start?
    The fall grid is dated from September 21, but the annual maintenance shutdown was posted as September 8–23. The calendar keeps the shutdown and starts the fall schedule on September 24.
  • GapThanksgiving and Remembrance Day
    The Holiday Hours page doesn't list hours for Thanksgiving (October 12) or Remembrance Day (November 11) yet, so the calendar shows the regular schedule on both days.
  • GapWhat's in the other lanes?
    When only three or four lanes are open for length swim, the grid doesn't say what is in the rest: lessons, clubs or rentals. Those would sit beside the lap lanes on the same calendar.
  • GapThe Leisure Pool and the dive tank
    The Leisure Pool has three swim lanes, and the Lap Pool comes “with dive tank”, but no lane grid is published for either, so neither is on the calendar yet. Is the dive tank separate water from the eight lanes?
  • OursWhich lanes are open
    The centre publishes how many lanes are open, not which ones, so the lane numbers on the calendar are our estimate: open lanes are drawn from lane 1.
